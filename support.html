A little help

Prismora support

Roll the prism across the board and place it upright over the square hole. Swipe up, down, left or right with one finger. You can enable the directional buttons in the pause menu.

How do I finish a level?

The prism must stand upright on the goal hole. Keep both halves supported when it lies flat. Use switches to open bridges and watch for fragile tiles.

How do hints refill?

You begin with three free hints. One free hint refills every 30 minutes, up to three. An optional Google AdMob rewarded ad gives one extra hint after its reward is earned. Timed free hints remain available when no ad can be loaded.

Can I undo a fall?

Yes. Undo returns to your previous valid position. Restart begins the same puzzle again.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and preferences are saved on your device. There is no account or developer cloud save. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may remove progress. Device backup behaviour depends on your operating system.

How do I change language or audio?

Open Settings from the menu. Choose one of eight languages and adjust Music and Game sounds separately.
