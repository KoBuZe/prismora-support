Prismora Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-06 · Türkçe

ADVERTISING
The Android game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Rewarded ads are optional and award one hint only when the ad reports that its reward was earned. Interstitial ads can appear at a break after three completed levels or seven failed attempts. Free hints recharge every 30 minutes, up to three, without an ad.

When ads are enabled, Google's advertising SDK can collect and share your IP address (used to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic information such as launch time, hangs and energy usage. Google uses these for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and SDK operation. SDK traffic is encrypted in transit. Data handling depends on consent, region and Google services; see policies.google.com/privacy and developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

ADVERTISING PROTECTION
Prismora's intended store audience is 13 and older. The game does not ask for or store your age or birthday. All ad requests receive Google's stronger under-age-of-consent treatment, disabling personalized and remarketing ads and restricting advertising identifiers. Ads use Google's G content-rating limit. The protective setting is applied before initializing the advertising SDK and requesting ads, for every player.

YOUR CHOICES
Where required, Google's consent message is shown before ads are requested. Settings > Privacy options is available when Google requires an entry point to review or change your advertising choices. If an ad is unavailable, the game remains playable. Closing an optional ad before earning its reward does not award a hint.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android's privacy settings. Clear Prismora's storage in Android Settings to delete local progress and preferences. Clearing local data does not delete data already held by Google; manage Google's privacy controls separately.

DEVICE ACCESS
Prismora does not request camera, microphone, contacts, GPS or storage-library access. Network access supports ads and consent. Optional vibration provides game feedback. There are no in-app purchases or account registration.

RETENTION AND CONTACT
Local saves remain until you clear them or uninstall the app. Google determines retention for data processed by its services. Contact Kobu Games at koraycakmakli@hotmail.com. A hosted policy URL will be supplied on the Google Play listing before distribution. This policy remains accessible offline from Settings.

This policy will be updated when the game's features or service providers change.

Website and support

The support website uses no developer tracking pixels, analytics or contact forms. GitHub Pages may process hosting requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Support emails are used to answer your request and retained as needed for support and legal obligations.
